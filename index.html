    • This project focuses on creating digital marketing graphics for Brew Haven, a coffee brand that promotes the cozy and comforting experience of enjoying coffee, especially during the autumn season. The design features a seasonal campaign highlighting the Pumpkin Spice Latte, using warm colors, coffee imagery, and autumn-inspired elements to create an inviting and appealing visual presentation.
    • The color palette consists of orange, yellow, cream, brown, and black, which helps communicate warmth, comfort, and the cozy atmosphere associated with fall. The use of bold typography, attractive product photography, and promotional phrases such as “Cozy Season, Perfect Reason” and “Try It Today” helps capture the audience's attention and encourage them to try the featured beverage.
    • The project includes different promotional layouts and graphic variations designed for digital platforms. Each design maintains a consistent brand identity while presenting the product in an engaging and visually appealing way. The combination of imagery, typography, color, and promotional messaging demonstrates how digital marketing graphics can communicate a brand's message effectively.

    Typography
    Brand Poster

    • This project focuses on creating a typography brand poster for Patience Fine Jewelry, designed to promote the brand's grand opening on September 16, 2026. The poster emphasizes elegance, luxury, and sophistication through the use of refined typography, a minimalist layout, and a dark color scheme with gold and white accents.
    • The design features the brand name as the main focal point, using elegant serif typography to communicate a luxurious and timeless identity. The gold text adds a sense of exclusivity, while the dark background enhances the contrast and creates a premium appearance. The tagline, “Where Patience Become Precious,” reinforces the brand's message and reflects the value and beauty of fine jewelry.
